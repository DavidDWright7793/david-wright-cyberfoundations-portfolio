## Email analysis
1. The sender of the email is asking the recipient to confirm their username and current password on a website they provide \(whose domain is different from that of the clinic's actual website\).
2. The email arrived from a domain \(clinic-support.example\) that is different from that of the clinic's designated scheduling vendor \(appointments-vendor.example\) and that of the clinic itself \(cloudheights-clinic.example\). The display name of the sender \("Clinic IT Support"\) is also typed by the sender.
3. The email contains a strong sense of urgency, as the subject line contains "URGENT" and states that the scheduling account will be closed today. The email body instructs the recipient to confirm their username and current password at the website they provide, and says that if they do not do so within 2 hours, the appointment system will be locked for the entire clinic for the rest of the day.

**Safe response / reporting step:** I would immediately report the suspicious email to IT/security through the clinic's designated reporting mechanism. I would also preserve the email as-is so IT/security can investigate it, and not modify or delete it before reporting unless clinic policy requires otherwise. If I forward the email itself to IT/security, I would do it through their official reporting channel. I would definitely tell IT/security staff about the email, as well as the manager/supervisor of my department. If any other employees needed to know about the suspicious email, I would allow the IT/security department or my department manager coordinate such communications.

**Suspicious vs proven:** There is reasonable proof that the message did not come from either the clinic staff or the clinic's designated scheduling vendor, since the email domain name of the sender differs from both of the other two domains.  I would need to check if the scheduling vendor had any policy of sending clients to a website to update their password, and if so, which website.  In fact, I would need to investigate what the proper procedure for users to update their password is. I would also need to check if the scheduling vendor had any policy of closing access to the scheduling account if a user does not update their password within a designated timeframe \(especially closing access to an entire clinic\).
