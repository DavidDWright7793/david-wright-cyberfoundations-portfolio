## Portfolio Deliverable 3 Reflection

Write 5–7 sentences. What can a correctly checked digital badge tell you about the service? What can it NOT promise? Describe one problem using the message you actually saw, and explain what you checked next. Compare the real website with your practice service. You may start with “I used to think…”, “My check showed…”, and “I now know…”.

```text
I used to believe that a valid digital certificate meant a website was completely safe. I now know that a correctly verified certificate confirms the website's domain name, public key, validity dates, and trusted issuer. However, it cannot guarantee that the website is free from vulnerabilities, its information is accurate, or its owners are honest. A real-life analogy is like that of a state-issued driver's license. The license verifies that the person holding it has met the requirements to be legally authorized to drive, and was issued the license to do so by the proper authority (the DMV). It does not guarantee that the person will always drive safely and always obey traffic laws.
```
