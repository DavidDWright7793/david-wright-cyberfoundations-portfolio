## What happened?
On September 21, 2026, five unsuccessful attempts were made to sign in to Casey Rivera's account from a device that was not registered with the organization. Each attempt failed because an incorrect password was entered, but a sixth sign-in attempt was successful. All six attempts occurred within three minutes, raising questions about whether the activity was legitimate or unauthorized.

## Why does it matter?
If an unauthorized individual gained access to Casey Rivera's account, they could potentially view or share confidential information and make unauthorized changes to important records. These changes could disrupt normal operations, delay work, and affect other employees who rely on the information. However, the available evidence does not establish that any information was accessed or altered without authorization or that any services were disrupted.

## What did we find?
The investigation confirmed that all six sign-in attempts came from the same device, which was not registered with the organization. However, the available information does not establish who made the attempts or whether the successful sign-in was authorized.

## What should happen next?
The next step is to confirm with Casey Rivera whether the successful sign-in was legitimate and review additional records to determine whether anyone accessed the account without permission. The investigation should also examine whether any information was viewed or altered after the successful sign-in.

## What remains uncertain
It remains unclear whether the successful sign-in was legitimate or whether an unauthorized individual gained access to Casey Rivera's account without permission. It is also unknown whether any confidential or sensitive information was viewed or shared, or whether any important records were altered.
