## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape is the overall picture of cybersecurity-the attack surface (every possible point in a system that can be attacked), the threat landscape (the overall picture of who is attacking, how they are attacking, and why they are attacking), and the digital infrastructure (the hardware, software, and network components of systems). The major players in the cybersecurity landscape are attackers (malicious hackers, cybercriminals), defenders (SOC analysts, GRC analysts, pentesters/ethical hackers), infrastructure, and everyday users. The who, how, and why of attacks are constantly changing, therefore the cybersecurity landscape is constantly evolving.
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
In a computer, the hardware, OS, and software layers interact in order to allow a user to input requests into the computer, and for the computer to produce the requested information for the user. The user inputs his/her request to an application (software), which then relays the request to the OS, which then communicates with the hardware. The hardware then produces the requested information, transmits it to the OS, which then relays it to the software application, when then displays it to the user.
```
